1. Quick Start & Portal Authentication

Admin Teacher Accountant Warden Student

Madeenathu CM Olavanna ERP operates on a role-based access model. Each user type sees only the permissions and menus relevant to their responsibilities.

1

Accessing the Portal

Open your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari) and navigate to the institution URL. Click Login to ERP on the landing page.

/login/
2

Entering Your Credentials

Enter your assigned Username and Password. If logging in for the first time as a student, use your Student Register Number.

3

Navigating the Workspace

Upon successful login, you are directed to the main Dashboard containing dynamic analytics cards and quick-action buttons for daily tasks.

Pro Tip: You can install this portal as a standalone app on your phone or PC by clicking the Install App button in Chrome/Safari without needing Google Play Store.

2. System Setup & Academic Configuration

Admin Only

Configure the foundational academic hierarchy of Madeenathu CM Olavanna (Academic Sessions, Sections, Classes, Divisions, and Subjects).

1

Configure Academic Years

Set up sessions (e.g. 2026-2027). Exactly one academic year is marked as Active per section to govern student promotions.

Sidebar → Settings → Academic Years
2

Create Sections & Grades

Establish Sections (Secondary, Higher Secondary, Shareea, Degree) and Grades (Plus One, Plus Two, 1st Year Degree).

Sidebar → Settings → Grades / Classes
3

Set up Divisions & Classrooms

Create divisions (e.g. Dawa Commerce, Dawa Humanities, Science) under their respective Grades.

Sidebar → Settings → Divisions
4

Manage Subjects

Add curriculum subjects and categorize them as Academic or Dawa / Islamic subjects.

Sidebar → Settings → Subjects

4. Smart Attendance System

Admin Teacher Warden

Record daily presence, period-wise class attendance, and activity sessions with instant percentage calculation.

1

Marking Attendance by Class

Select the Grade and Division. The entire class roll list is loaded with all students defaulted to Present. Simply tap on any absent student to mark them Absent, Late, or Excused.

Attendance → Mark Attendance

5. Hostel Movement & Gate Pass (Outpass)

Admin Warden

Digital gate-pass registry to log hostel departures, escorting guardians, reasons, and return timestamps with overdue return alerts.

1

Creating an Outpass / Departure Record

Click Record Departure. Select the student, enter departure date & time, escort person name, contact number, reason, and expected return date.

Hostel → Record Movement
2

Logging Arrival (Return Check-in)

When the student returns to campus, locate their pending entry on the Hostel dashboard and click Record Arrival to close the outpass ticket.

3

Live Campus Roll-Call

The dashboard displays real-time badges showing how many hostel muthallimeen are currently In Campus vs. On Leave.

10. Installing the Mobile Web App (PWA)

Admin Teacher Accountant Warden Student

How to install Madeenathu CM Olavanna ERP as a fast, native-like app on Android phones, iPhones, iPads, and Windows PCs.

Android (Google Chrome)

1. Open the portal in Chrome.
2. Tap the three dots menu (⋮) at top-right.
3. Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
4. The app icon appears instantly on your launcher.

iPhone / iPad (Safari)

1. Open the portal in Safari.
2. Tap the Share button (⎋ / square with arrow) at the bottom.
3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
4. Tap Add.

Windows PC / Mac (Chrome / Edge)

1. Open the portal in Chrome or Edge.
2. Click the Install Icon (⊕) on the right side of the address bar.
3. Click Install to launch as a standalone desktop application.
